The problem

Recruitment has enough data.
It needs more context.

Our business-plan research identified three recurring gaps. HRMS-AI was designed to bridge them in a way recruiters and hiring managers can both understand.

More than keyword matching

A CV can contain the right words without demonstrating the capabilities that make someone effective in this specific role.

Align with the hiring manager

Recruiters need the manager's definition of success up front—not hidden expectations discovered after the shortlist is ready.

Walk into interviews prepared

Interviewers benefit from a simple, tailored briefing: what the applicant has demonstrated, what remains unclear and what to ask next.

        About HRMS-AI

        Technology that informs. People who decide.

        HRMS-AI began as the AI-Powered Recruitment Intelligence Platform business-plan project developed by Group C7 of the IIM Kozhikode EPGP-17 programme. The proposed product focuses on a simple principle: help interviewers understand the individual and the job better, while leaving employment decisions to humans.

        Take another look
        Questions people ask

        Clear answers about the demo.

        Is this a working AI recruitment product?

        Not yet. This is an interactive front-end prototype showing the proposed experience. It calculates explainable example scores from fictional candidate skill values. It does not process actual résumés or call a machine-learning service.

        What makes the idea different from applicant tracking?

        HRMS-AI starts with company mission and hiring-manager success criteria, then uses that role context to create an evidence-driven shortlist and candidate-specific interviewer brief. The longer-term idea is an intelligence layer that can work alongside existing ATS software, rather than forcing a replacement.

        Does the platform decide who gets hired?

        No. The project's guiding principle is Human Final Decision: the system provides information for consideration, and qualified people make the decision. Scores are not intended to determine hiring outcomes by themselves.

        Can I try it without registering or sharing my CV?

        Yes. Every interaction works locally in the page with sample data. You can change the hiring scenario, context, skill weights and pipeline stage. Refreshing the page restores the default experience.
