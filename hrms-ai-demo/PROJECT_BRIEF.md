# HRMS-AI — ChatGPT Project brief

**Project name:** HRMS-AI — Recruitment Intelligence Demo

**Objective:** Develop and publicly demonstrate the HRMS-AI concept as a polished, easy-to-understand interactive website for recruiters, hiring managers, candidates, collaborators and curious visitors. This is NOT a fundraising website.

**Core insight from the source business plan:** Recruiters and ATS products often screen applicants without capturing what success means for the company and hiring manager. HRMS-AI begins with organisation mission/values and role context, then provides skill-gap analysis and tailored interviewer questions. Hiring decisions remain human-controlled.

**What currently works:** Six-step client-side journey, 3 role scenarios, editable criteria, deterministic weighted scoring, fictional candidate profiles, printable executive report, human-managed pipeline, and synced candidate status. Works offline; no backend or live AI.

**Current boundaries:** Use synthetic data and label it. Do not send data externally, claim actual screening accuracy, or imply that digital footprint/background checks are performed. The report's onboarding and performance modules are planned future capabilities.

**Current files:** index.html, styles.css, app.js, README.md, AGENTS.md, tests/smoke.py, deployment configuration.

**Suggested Codex starting prompt:** "Read AGENTS.md and PROJECT_BRIEF.md. Preserve the current six-step public demo and the Human Final Decision principle. Run the smoke checks, fix any regressions, then propose scoped improvements to accessibility, polished visualizations and the recruitment workflow. Keep all applicant data fictional and make no claims about live AI functionality."
